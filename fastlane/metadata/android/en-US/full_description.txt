Netless is an open-source Android app that brings UPI payments to users with no internet connection, using cellular rails already supported on Indian phones: *99# USSD and UPI 123Pay (IVR).

<b>Why Netless?</b>
When cellular data drops, standard UPI apps fail. Netless wraps existing offline telecom rails behind a clean, smartphone-native interface:
• <b>Scan QR:</b> Scans merchant UPI QR codes via CameraX and ZXing, dialing the *99*1*3# USSD payment rail.
• <b>Pay Contact:</b> Initiates a UPI 123Pay IVR call carrying the payee and amount via DTMF, with an on-screen guide.
• <b>Dual-Rail Architecture:</b> Works across all major operators. Jio users benefit from the 123Pay rail, while GSM carriers support *99# USSD.

<b>Privacy & Security:</b>
• <b>No Internet Permission:</b> The app does not request or hold android.permission.INTERNET. It cannot communicate over IP or leak data.
• <b>Never Sees Your UPI PIN:</b> You enter your PIN directly into your issuer bank's official USSD or IVR prompt. Netless uses no accessibility service.
• <b>Local Encrypted Storage:</b> Transaction history is stored locally in an encrypted database using SQLCipher.
• <b>Zero Analytics or Trackers:</b> 100% Free and Open Source Software (FOSS) licensed under Apache-2.0.
